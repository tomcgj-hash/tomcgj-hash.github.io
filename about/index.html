About

One headend line,
documented model by model

This site is a working catalogue of the FMUSER FUQ digital TV headend line: 191 models across 9 categories, each one published with the manufacturer datasheet that carries its inputs, formats, dimensions and power. Nothing on a model page is written from memory — it is taken from that document.

We are an export desk, not a photo booth: the pages show the equipment, the specifications behind it, and the way a project gets specified. Commercial terms are confirmed in writing per order.

How an enquiry runs

01

Send the requirement

Channel count, input sources, output standard, rack space and the market you deliver to. A photo of the rack or a channel list is enough to start.

02

Model list and channel plan

We come back with the specific models, how they connect, and what each one does in the chain — not a generic brochure list.

03

Confirm against the datasheet

Every parameter on the model pages comes from the manufacturer datasheet shipped with that model. If a detail is not in it, we ask the factory rather than guess.

04

Order, shipping and support

Quotation, payment terms and freight options are confirmed in writing, with technical support on the models supplied.

Confirmed per order, not claimed on a web page
