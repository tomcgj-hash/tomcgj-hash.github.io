FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from our factory.

01Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?
We are a manufacturer. All our equipment is designed, developed and produced in our own ISO 9001 certified factory in Guangzhou, China. You buy directly from the source — no middlemen.
02What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ is 1 unit for most standard products. For OEM/ODM customization (custom branding, specifications or packaging), a minimum order applies — contact us for details.
03How fast will I receive a quotation?
Standard quotations are sent within 24 hours. For complex projects with custom requirements, our engineers may need 2–3 days to prepare a full proposal.
04What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer (T/T), PayPal and major credit cards for sample orders, and T/T or Letter of Credit for bulk orders. Full payment terms are provided with each quotation.
05How long does production and delivery take?
Standard products ship within 3–7 days. Bulk orders typically take 15–30 days depending on quantity. We offer both air freight (3–7 days) and sea freight (20–40 days) worldwide.
06Do you provide OEM / ODM services?
Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM services including custom branding, custom specifications, software customization and custom packaging for your product line.
07What warranty do you provide?
All products come with a standard 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranties and spare parts are available on request.
08Do you provide technical support after purchase?
Yes — every order includes lifetime technical support. Our engineers provide remote diagnostics, configuration assistance and on-site support where required.
09Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?
Absolutely. Sample orders are welcome and we recommend testing equipment with your own system before scaling up.
