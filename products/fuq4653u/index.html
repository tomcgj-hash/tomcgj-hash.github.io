FUQ4653U 4K Encoder Modulator Dual HDMI

The FMUSER FUQ-4653U is a professional 4K encoder modulator supporting dual HDMI 2.0, 2xASI, and 32xIP inputs with DVB-C/T RF, ASI, and IP outputs. It performs H.265/HEVC encoding and DVB modulation in a 1U rack-mountable chassis. The device features web-based NMS for remote control and supports PSI/SI
