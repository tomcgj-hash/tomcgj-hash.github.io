FUQ4443 FUQ-4443 IP QAM Modulator 1024 IP Inputs 32 QAM

The FMUSER FUQ-4443 is an all-in-one IP QAM modulator integrating multiplexing, scrambling, and modulation, with three GE ports for up to 1024 IP inputs and a single RF interface for 32 QAM outputs. It processes signals by encoding and modulating them into DVB-C compliant formats. The device supports web-based network management and RS (204,188) encoding. Ideal for modern CATV broadcasting systems, it ensures scalability and high performance in cable TV headends and digital video distribution.
