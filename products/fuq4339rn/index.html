FUQ4339RN IP Streaming Encoder 24 HDMI

The FMUSER FUQ-4339R-N is a multi-channel network encoder with up to 24 HDMI inputs, supporting H.264 video and AAC audio encoding for IP broadcasting. It processes signals by encoding and streaming via protocols like UDP, SRT, RTMP, and HLS over internet/LAN. Featuring web-based management for control and updates. Ideal for applications in live event streaming to PCs, smartphones, and mobile devices.
