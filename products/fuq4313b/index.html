FUQ4313B FUQ-4313B Dual Channel Audio Encoder Analog

The FMUSER FUQ-4313B is a dual-channel audio encoder supporting analog balanced audio and AES/EBU digital audio inputs via XLR interfaces, with outputs for ASI multiplexing and IP/UDP/RTP. It encodes audio signals using MPEG1 Layer 2 and AAC formats. Control is available via LCD/keyboard or Web GUI, featuring hot-pluggable dual/single PSU options. Ideal for digital broadcasting and robust broadcast workflows.
