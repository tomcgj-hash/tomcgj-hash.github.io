Multiplexer / Scrambler

FUQ4823C FUQ-4823C Multiplexer Scrambler 8 ASI Inputs 64 IP Inputs UD

The FMUSER FUQ-100B is a multiplexer and scrambler supporting 8 ASI and 64 IP inputs. It processes signals with PID re-mapping and EPG insertion, outputting TS streams via ASI and IP ports. With web-based NMS management, it suits digital TV head-end systems and CATV broadcasting.
