Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Every new FMUSER purchase order is covered by our 7-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with the service for any reason within 7 days of your purchase, contact our support team and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply:

  1. Contact our support team by email or WhatsApp.
  2. Provide your account email address and order details.
  3. Tell us what went wrong — your feedback helps us improve.

Refund requests are processed within 5–10 business days and the amount is returned to your original payment method.

Exceptions

The following cases are not eligible for a refund:

  • Subscriptions purchased more than 7 days ago.
  • Renewal charges after the guarantee period, unless the service was unavailable.
  • Violations of our Terms of Service, including account sharing beyond the allowed connections.
